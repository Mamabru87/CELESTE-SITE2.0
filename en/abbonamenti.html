Floral subscriptions and Vase en Résidence in Milan

Beauty on a fixed schedule.

CÉLESTE designs floral compositions for private homes, hotels, restaurants and showrooms in Milan, delivered and renewed on a schedule agreed with each client.

Vase en Résidence:

A dialogue between the vessel, the space and the flowers. The studio's élite service: the composition is conceived to inhabit the client's space, following its proportions, light and materials, until it becomes part of the place.

Subscriptions:

Compositions renewed at regular intervals, always and only with seasonal flowers. Each delivery changes with nature, while the identity of the space stays consistent over time.

For whom:

Private clients, hotels, restaurants and showrooms in Milan. Formats, frequency and budget are defined together, by appointment: every subscription is bespoke.

For a single occasion, discover the Aria and Moiré collections; for weddings and events, our art direction.
