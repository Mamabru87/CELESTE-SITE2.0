Wedding and event flowers in Milan

Art direction: botanical stagings.

At CÉLESTE, a place is not decorated: it is inhabited. The studio's art direction turns a space into a botanical staging, designing form, colour and matter together with the architecture and light that hold them.

Weddings:

From the bouquet to the installations, every wedding is a bespoke project, in Milan and across Lombardy. We work only with seasonal flowers: the palette is drawn from what nature offers at that moment, for a result that is unique and cannot be replicated.

Events, windows, sets:

Botanical stagings for private events, window displays, terraces and photo sets. Compositions that move between the ethereal and a bold, textural presence, conceived for the place and the time of the event.

How we work:

CÉLESTE has no shop open to the public: it is a nomadic studio working exclusively on commission and by appointment. Every project starts from a brief (date, venue, atmosphere) and the quote is tailored to it.
